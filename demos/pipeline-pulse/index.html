Why it matters

The operating model behind the outcomes.

Proves

DMBOK becomes practical when knowledge areas are implemented as platform controls — reusable DQ rules, lineage, stewardship queues, audit evidence and AI governance — not separate dashboards. This is that operating model rendered as clickable software.

Why it matters to enterprises

It gives a CDO, steward or auditor one shared, defensible picture: what is onboarded, who owns it, what passed quality, which privacy rules apply, what evidence exists, and how incidents become prevention controls.
