About

About Ram Balasubrahmanian.

I help regulated enterprises turn fragmented data operations into trusted operating systems: governed, observable, audit-ready, and built for AI adoption. At FICO, and earlier at EY, I have worked where engineering reliability, governance control, regulatory evidence, and business adoption meet.

At FICO I scaled data operations for 50+ global clients across banking, insurance, retail and telecom, including Fortune 500 companies in North America, the UK and Australia; reduced production incidents by 95% in three months, from about 130 to under 10 per quarter, and MTTR from 50–100 hours to 4–20 hours (most resolved in under 4); protected 232M+ regulated records per extraction cycle; delivered $2M+ annual savings through the Defect Review Board, incident reduction and ServiceNow automation across FICO’s data platforms and data management platforms; grew platform ARR by $10M+; and compressed onboarding from 4 weeks to 2–3 days with metadata-driven quality controls — managing a 25+ member team with 11 direct reports across the US, Canada and India, directing a wider cross-functional group of 50+, hiring 30+ people across 8+ years at FICO, and owning governance for multiple data services: data governance, data quality and data management fundamentals.

Since April 2025 I’ve run an independent data & AI governance practice — an Enterprise Data Steward engagement via Toptal, reporting directly to the client’s CDO and CTO on data governance, metadata and data quality, with the business glossary, data catalog and lineage in Microsoft Purview and Collibra and data issue workflows in ServiceNow; governed-RAG demos I built myself; and a LinkedIn newsletter to 3,000+ subscribers. From October 2026 I also serve as Officer of Education for the DAMA India Bengaluru Chapter, shaping the chapter’s workshops and webinars, and training and mentoring CDMP candidates across Data Management Fundamentals, Data Governance and Data Quality through to the Master track. I build governed AI, not just advise on it. See independent-practice deliverables.

I lead data governance, data quality, DataOps, metadata and AI-control programmes for BFSI and other data-intensive organisations. Based in Bengaluru (Bangalore), working with teams across India, the GCC, Europe and APAC.
